The tracked copy of the evidence behind CURRENT.md. Raw outputs live under the ignored `Artifacts/Results/cluster_{rf01,ts01,rt01}_*` and on Atlantis under `research_runs/<RUN>/source/...`.

## Starting point
RB07: three development seeds (101/202/303), each trained to 401,408,000 steps on the pinned MuJoCo Playground G1 joystick task (MJX, flat terrain, 2048 envs, lr 3e-4). None passed all original gates. Failures were randomized-reset foot-foot/foot-shin contact terminations while upright, plus nominal turning yaw .36-.40 against a .25 limit.

## RF01 - narrow-foot penalty (comparison 1 of 2): REJECTED
Tested narrow_feet -4 (width .16m) against a matched control, each continued 51.6M steps at lr 3e-4.
- 303 fix had 3 foot-contact terminations against 1 in its control.
- Both 202 arms had 0.
- 101 ctl was stopped by the KL guard (kl 11.25).
- 101 fix froze at compile and was cancelled (job 7983).
- Every arm failed nominal turning, which made turning the shared blocker.

## TS01 - turn-command sweep (evaluation only): CONFIRMED
Seven policies were tested: the RB07 finals and the RF01 202/303 arms. Every one stands still (single support .00) for small pure-yaw commands:

| pure yaw | result in 7/7 policies |
|---|---|
| ±.2, ±.3 | stands still, yaw error = command |
| ±.4 (the gate command) | flips by policy and direction |
| ±.6, ±1.0 | steps and tracks (error .06-.17) |
| ±.4 plus forward speed .1-.45 | mostly steps and tracks (about .1) |

Mechanism: the upstream yaw reward is 0.75*exp(-err^2/0.25). Standing through yaw .3 still earns 70% of it (53% at .4, 24% at .6), so for small commands standing is cheaper than paying the stepping costs. Zero-linear turns are also only about 1% of upstream command draws.

## RT01 - stricter yaw reward + in-place turn commands (comparison 2 of 2): REJECTED
Arms, each continued 51.6M steps from the RB07 finals:
- Fix: yaw-rate tracking sigma .05 (new adapter option), plus 15% of moving command draws replaced by zero-linear turns with |yaw| in [.2,1.0].
- Control: upstream reward and command sampler.
- Both arms: lr 1e-4.

Rule check:
- (1) All three fix arms pass the nominal turn gates.
- (2) Unmeasurable as frozen: the reused grid has ±.2/.4/.6/1.0 but no ±.3. That was an authoring error. At ±.2 every arm, fix included, still stands.
- (3) Fails: 101 fix has randomized falls and 3 foot-contact terminations that its control lacks.

Verdict: REJECT, with no extension or retry. Both approved comparisons are now used, and the mandatory review is due.

Findings worth carrying forward:
- The stricter yaw reward moved the dead zone from about ≤.4 down to about ≤.2. Every fix arm now steps and tracks at ±.4, and the fix fixed nominal turning on all three seeds.
- 101 ctl (lr 1e-4 continuation, no reward change) passed every numeric gate in every evaluation. That is a single seed, still needs visual review, and is not final validation.
- At lr 1e-4 no arm hit the KL guard. At 3e-4, RF01 101 ctl blew up.
- The 101 fix regression is in randomized recovery, so the stricter yaw term may trade off against recovery.
- The 303 ctl evaluation can be recovered from its saved 51.6M checkpoint without retraining; it would not change the verdict.
